Connect your Gmail

  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification in your Google Account (Security).
  2. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords, create an App Password named "NextGen Apply" and copy the 16 letters.
  3. In the project's .env set SMTP_USER to your Gmail address and SMTP_PASS to the App Password.
  4. Restart the backend. The inbox is checked every few minutes.

The App Password only works for this app and can be revoked any time from the same Google page.
